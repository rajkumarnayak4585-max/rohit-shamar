ABOUT ROHIT

Rohit Sharma is an Indian international cricketer, born on 30 April 1987 in Bansod, Nagpur, Maharashtra. He is famous for his stylish batting, powerful sixes and excellent timing. Fans call him "Hitman". His journey inspires young cricket lovers around the world.

CRICKET CAREER

Some important highlights from Rohit Sharma's cricket journey.

🏏 Batting Style

Right-handed batter known for timing, elegant shots and powerful six-hitting.

🌍 International Cricket

Represented India in Test cricket, One Day Internationals and T20 Internationals.

👑 Captaincy

Led India in major international tournaments and helped the team win the 2024 T20 World Cup.

💥 Batting Records

Holds the men's ODI individual highest-score record with 264 runs in one innings.
